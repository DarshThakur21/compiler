
Generate the Parser (creates parser.tab.cc, parser.tab.hh, and location.hh):

bison -d parser.yy -o parser.tab.cc

Generate the Lexer (creates lexer.cc):
re2c -o lexer.cc lexer.re




Compile the Executable:
g++ -std=c++17 main.cpp parser.tab.cc lexer.cc -o conj_compiler


Run the Compiler on your input script:
.\conj_compiler.exe test.conj
